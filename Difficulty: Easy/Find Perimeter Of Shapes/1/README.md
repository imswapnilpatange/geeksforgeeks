<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/find-perimeter-of-shapes/1">Find Perimeter Of Shapes/1</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a binary matrix <strong data-end="102" data-start="91">mat[][]</strong> of size <strong data-end="120" data-start="111">n × m</strong>, where each cell contains either <strong data-end="159" data-start="154">0</strong> or <strong data-end="168" data-start="163">1</strong>, find the total perimeter of all figures formed by cells containing <strong data-end="247" data-start="241">1s</strong>. Two cells are considered adjacent if they share a common side.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 20px;">A single cell containing 1 has a perimeter of 4, whereas two adjacent cells containing 1 (i.e., 11) together have a perimeter of 6.</span></p>
