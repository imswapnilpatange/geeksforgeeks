<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/biggest-integer-having-maximum-digit-sum1704/1">Biggest Integer Having Maximum Digit Sum1704/1</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a number <strong>n</strong>, find a number in the range from 1 to <strong>n </strong>such that its digit sum is maximum. If there are multiple such numbers, return the largest of them.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input</strong>: n = 48</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">
<strong>Output:</strong> 48
<strong>Explanation</strong>: There are two numbers with maximum digit sum = 12. The numbers are 48 and 39. Since 48 &gt; 39, so 48 is the answer.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 90
<strong>Output: </strong>89<br/><strong>Explanation</strong>: 89 gives us the largest digit sum in the range from 1 to n. Hence the answer is 89. </span></pre></div>
